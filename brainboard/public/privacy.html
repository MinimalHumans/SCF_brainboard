Scriptyard

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

The short version: your work stays on your device. Scriptyard has no backend, no accounts, and no analytics. If you turn on Google Drive sync, your boards go into a private folder in your own Drive — we never see them. The only data that ever reaches us is the standard access log any web server keeps.

Scriptyard is a story-planning board tool published by Minimal Humans (“Minimal Humans,” “we,” “us”). This policy covers the Scriptyard web app at scriptyard.minimalhumans.com and explains what data Scriptyard handles and how, so you can make an informed decision about using it.

Data stored on your device

Your boards and templates — cards, layout, and related content — are stored locally in your browser using the Origin Private File System (OPFS). This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server we operate. We do not run our own backend and have no access to your board or template content.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to enable sync, Scriptyard uses the Google Drive API’s application data folder (the drive.appdata scope) to back up your local boards and templates to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. This lets you carry your work across your own devices. Specifically:

  • Only your board and template files (the same content already stored locally via OPFS) are written to the app data folder.
  • The app data folder is private to Scriptyard — it does not appear in your regular Drive file list, and Scriptyard cannot see, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
  • We have no server-side access to this data. It moves directly between your browser and your Google account via Google’s API; Scriptyard has no backend that stores, logs, or processes it.
  • You can disconnect sync at any time from inside Scriptyard, or revoke Scriptyard’s access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Neither action deletes the boards already stored locally on your device.
  • To also delete the synced copies from your Google account, open Google Drive’s settings → Manage apps → Scriptyard → Delete hidden app data.

Google’s own handling of data you store via its APIs is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Server access logs

Like virtually every website, the web server that delivers the Scriptyard app records standard access logs: the IP address, browser user-agent, timestamp, and URL of each request. We use these logs solely to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. They are not linked to your boards or your Google account, are not used for tracking or profiling, are not shared with anyone, and are deleted in the course of routine log rotation.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect analytics or usage tracking.
  • We don’t use cookies for tracking purposes.
  • We don’t require or offer any account system beyond the optional Google sign-in used for Drive sync.
  • We don’t sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, and we don’t use your data for advertising.
  • We don’t collect crash reports or device identifiers.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Scriptyard’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data accessed through the Google Drive API is used solely to provide the boards and templates sync feature described above and is not used for any other purpose.

Children’s privacy

Scriptyard is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update the effective date above and post the revised version at this same URL.
